The story behind my name

A journey from hiding to building.

When I first came to the U.S. at 26, I struggled with English, so much that even my own name felt like a barrier.

I worried that Inkyu would be hard to pronounce, easy to forget, or too foreign to belong. A pastor who welcomed me gave me an English name: Joseph. For years, Joseph became my name in every room where I needed to blend in.

But as I kept building my life, my work, and my systems, I realized something.

The name I once tried to hide is now the root of everything I build.

Inkyu is not a footnote to my story. It is the foundation. It carries my origin, my culture, my family’s hopes, and my own decision to begin again on my terms.

Joseph Inkyu Yu is a statement of where I come from, what I’ve built, and who I am still becoming. Not to erase where I started, but to integrate it.

I no longer hide my name. I lead with it.
